From Data to Decisions

Interactive Dashboards for Museum Engagement.

Helping the Ann Arbor Hands-On Museum transform attendance, membership, and donor data into actionable insights.

Overview

Background

The Ann Arbor Hands-On Museum serves over 300,000 visitors each year. Museum staff wanted an easier way to understand visitor engagement, membership activity, and donor trends so they could make more informed decisions.

Problem

Challenge

The museum's attendance, membership, and donor information was spread across multiple datasets.

This made it difficult to monitor visitor trends, understand membership growth, identify donor opportunities, and support outreach decisions.

Goal

Project Objective

  • Monitor memberships
  • Analyze donor behavior
  • Support data-driven decision making

Dashboard 1

Donor Recency Dashboard

What it shows: donor frequency, net donations, days since last gift, and groups that may need reactivation.

Key Insight: The data shows a strong active base and a long-term lapse segment that may benefit from reactivation efforts.

Dashboard 2

Membership Dashboard

What it shows: memberships month to date, sales channels, and overlap between members and donors.

Key Insight: Most members purchase in person and do not contribute outside membership costs, creating an opportunity for donor growth.

Dashboard 3

Attendance Dashboard

What it shows: family events, school attendance, field trip attendance, seasonal patterns, and year-over-year trends.

Key Insight: The museum can prioritize high-yield family programming while tracking an upward field trip trend into May 2026.

Design Decisions

Why these dashboards?

  • Simplify complex data
  • Reduce visual clutter
  • Prioritize the most important KPIs
  • Make filtering intuitive
  • Support staff with different levels of technical experience
