SkillBridge

Connecting students with career-aligned volunteer opportunities.

SkillBridge is a proposed digital platform that helps students find volunteer opportunities related to their academic and career interests while helping employers connect with motivated early-career talent.

01 · Project overview

Experience should connect to where students want to go.

Students often struggle to find meaningful volunteer opportunities connected to their field of study. This can limit real-world experience, reduce their understanding of employer expectations, and affect confidence when preparing for careers.

SkillBridge was designed as a centralized platform where students could discover career-relevant roles and employers could post opportunities for early-career talent.

The design question

How might we help students find volunteer opportunities that build relevant experience while helping employers connect with motivated early-career contributors?

05 · Proposed solution

One platform. Two pathways.

A searchable, accessible experience that makes the connection between volunteering and career growth visible.

For students

  • Search by skill or career area
  • View personalized recommendations
  • Apply for or save roles
  • Track career-readiness progress
  • Showcase interests and skills

For organizations

  • Post volunteer opportunities
  • Reach students with relevant interests
  • Build early-talent relationships
  • Review engagement and application data

Assumptions
