Leadership · Campus life

Leadership &
Campus Roles

Supporting students, building community, and helping create spaces where people feel welcomed.

During my academic journey, I have held several leadership roles that allowed me to advocate for students, contribute to campus life, and grow as a collaborative leader.

Michigan Caregivers and Student Parents

Leading with empathy.

As Vice President of Michigan Caregivers and Student Parents (MCaSP), I supported a community of students balancing their education with caregiving responsibilities. This experience strengthened my ability to listen, advocate, organize, and lead with empathy.

UMSI Student Ambassador

Helping students find their place.

As a UMSI Student Ambassador, I helped prospective and current students learn more about the School of Information. I enjoyed sharing my experience, answering questions, and helping others feel confident navigating their academic journey.

Black@SI

Building an inclusive community.

My involvement with Black@SI gave me opportunities to connect with peers, support community programming, and contribute to a welcoming environment within UMSI.

Leadership has taught me that meaningful impact begins with listening, showing up, and making space for others.
