Mariette Bondey
Website

From an idea to a live website for a small business in the beauty and hair industry.

I designed and developed a clean, welcoming website that reflects the business and helps clients explore its services and connect with Mariette Bondey.

What I built

A welcoming digital home for a small business.

I planned the structure and navigation, created connected pages, established the visual style and color palette, and organized the services and business information into a clear user experience.

What I worked on

From structure to deployment.

  • Planned the website structure and navigation
  • Created multiple connected HTML pages
  • Designed the visual style and color palette
  • Built responsive layouts with HTML and CSS
  • Organized services and business information
  • Designed the contact and booking experience
  • Connected forms for customer inquiries
  • Used GitHub for version control
  • Deployed the website and connected a custom domain

What I learned

Building beyond the code.

This project gave me experience taking a website from concept to deployment. I learned that successful web development also requires understanding the client's needs, organizing information clearly, designing for users, testing the experience, and improving it over time.

Building this website helped me strengthen my web development and user-centered design skills while gaining experience creating a digital solution for a real small business.
